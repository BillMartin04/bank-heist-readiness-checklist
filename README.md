# Bank Heist Readiness Checklist

Four board questions and ten controls drawn from three North Korean heists: Bangladesh Bank (trusted access), Bybit (a trusted vendor's screen) and the Texas laptop farms (trusted people).

**[Download the checklist (PDF)](https://github.com/BillMartin04/bank-heist-readiness-checklist/releases/latest/download/Bank-Heist-Readiness-Checklist.pdf)**

Companion to the TechTalk with Bill video "How North Korea's Hackers Outsmarted the World's Banks and Stole $6.75 Billion."

## What is inside

1. **The four board questions:** What can we lose? How likely is it? Who owns it? Can we prove the control works?
2. **Ten controls**, each scored Yes, Partly or No, with an owner and a due date.
3. **Your result:** what your score means and what to fix first.

You can also read the checklist online in [checklist.md](checklist.md).
